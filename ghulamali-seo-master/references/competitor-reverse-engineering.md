# Competitor reverse-engineering: copy the strategy, not the content

Source: "The Only Video You Need to Watch" (13K views), plus the competitor-check parts of "Give me 9min..." and "Instant Google Ranking".

## The claim

"Pick any website that is already getting traffic from Google and you get a ready-made SEO strategy." A newcomer can skip months of research by studying what ranking competitors built. **You copy the keyword and page strategy. You do not copy their text.**

## Which competitors to study

His formula: look at the competitor's **DR (Ahrefs domain rating)**. If it is low (about 10 to 20), that site is beatable and its pages are worth copying. Study **5, 10, 20 or even 30** competitors, not one.

## Step by step

1. Put the competitor URL into a backlink/keyword tool (he uses Ahrefs Site Explorer). Read the overview: organic traffic, number of ranking keywords, referring domains, DR.
2. Open **Organic keywords / Top pages**. List every page and its main keyword. His example site had 29 pages and about 1,100 monthly visits, so the whole strategy fits on one screen.
3. For each page, note: the target keyword, the search volume, and how many other keywords the page also ranks for. His example: the homepage targeted "London printing" (about 150 searches) and got about 937 visits because it also ranked for related variants.
4. Open the live site. The **menu and link structure is the strategy**: parent services, child services, audience or location pages. Reproduce the structure for your own business.
5. Check titles, headings, and word count. In his example the pages were under about 500 words each.
6. **Merge across competitors.** Five competitors with 15 to 30 pages each gives you dozens of proven page ideas with the research already done. Cluster them by intent ([topical-mapping-and-intent.md](topical-mapping-and-intent.md)).
7. Build your page list, then write your own content, your own contact form, your own offer.

## What to learn from their backlinks

- Look at **referring domains versus DR**. 118 referring domains with a DR of 40 to 70 means strong links; thousands of referring domains with a DR in single digits means garbage links and a weak site that can be beaten.
- Filter to **dofollow** and **content (in-body) links**, then sort by DR to see their best links. In his example the best was DR 37, which is not hard to match.
- To replicate: contact those same sites for a placement, or buy placements from real, relevant sites. Do not copy a link that comes from a spam site.

## Content minimums he gives

- 300 words is the floor to avoid thin-content issues; aim for about **500 words** on a service page.
- Get the first batch written well (a professional, or a strong AI process) so the ranking test result is trustworthy. His warning: if you publish rubbish, wait 3 to 6 months, and conclude SEO does not work, you learned nothing.
