# Local industry playbooks (real estate, restaurants)

How he adapts the same method to a specific local industry. Each section is one video, read from YouTube's transcript panel in the original Hindi and translated by hand. His figures are claims from his own clients and the Pakistani market, not verified; swap in your own country's numbers. The generic method lives in [local-seo-keywords.md](local-seo-keywords.md), [google-business-profile.md](google-business-profile.md) and [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md); this file only adds what is specific to each industry.

## Real estate (`Kyf6zDRSm04`, "Real Estate Business SEO Strategy", 2026)

**His claims:** a DHA Lahore property office, already a decent business, wanted to grow with digital marketing. After SEO the site produced **147 qualified leads within six months**. He cites Pakistan's real-estate market at PKR 2 to 3 trillion and a large listing portal at 3 million organic visits a month, so "that traffic goes to your competitors". About **80% of buyers start on Google**, "property for sale in Lahore" gets 22,000-plus searches a month, **54% of property listing views are on mobile**, and one lead can be worth lakhs to crores. His illustration, which is arithmetic and not a forecast: 1% of 3 million is 30,000 visitors, and 1% of those converting is 300 leads a month.

He says "six steps" and then walks through seven topics; they are in his order below.

### 1. Research: low-competition, high-intent, local

- **Skip the head terms** ("property in Pakistan", "real estate Pakistan"). The big portals own them.
- His comparison: "property for sale" has about 49,000 searches and heavy competition; **"5 marla house for sale in DHA Phase 6 Lahore" has about 400 searches and no competition**. The second is better because the person typing it has already decided to buy. If your page is number one, that person calls.
- His filter pipeline in Ahrefs (the same as [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md)): Keywords Explorer, seed word, country, **Matching terms** (about 36,000 in his demo), **lowest DR 5 in the top 5**, **word count minimum 4**.
- **His four signs of a good keyword:** (1) at least one low-DR site in the top 5; (2) one irrelevant result in the top 5; (3) location-specific; (4) buyer intent.
- You usually do not need many backlinks for these; content optimisation alone can rank them.
- *Skipped:* the cheap shared tool seat he mentions. Use free tiers, Search Console and Google autocomplete instead.

### 2. Entity and Google Business Profile

Most property dealers either do not know about the profile or set it up badly, and miss business because of it. Steps: business category, service area, photos, **genuine reviews from real clients** (the most important part), weekly posts with new listings. The payoff is two chances to appear: your site in the normal results **and** your profile in the map for "property dealer near me". Full checklist and the suspension traps (never put keywords or locations in the business name) are in [google-business-profile.md](google-business-profile.md).

### 3. Authority content: three kinds, matched to the buyer's stage

| Kind | Example titles (his) | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Informational** | how to buy property in DHA Phase 6; 5 marla vs 10 marla; property tax rules | guide the buyer, earn trust |
| **Commercial** | best housing societies in Islamabad 2026; Bahria Town vs DHA, which is the better investment | help them compare |
| **Transactional** | 5 marla house for sale DHA Phase 6 Lahore; 1 kanal plot for sale Bahria Town Rawalpindi | direct enquiry |

To generate the most sales, research **transactional** keywords first and build pages for them; informational and commercial pages support those. See [topical-mapping-and-intent.md](topical-mapping-and-intent.md).

**Semantic writing:** do not repeat the keyword 50 to 100 times. Write on the topic and the related terms should arrive naturally. His list for "how to buy property in Pakistan": loan, registry, mutation, stamp duty, society, NOC, fard, title, deed. These are the terms that tell Google a real expert wrote it. See [on-page-and-semantic-content.md](on-page-and-semantic-content.md).

### 4. Location pages

One page per area you serve, in a hierarchy: **city, then district or society, then phase, then property type** (Lahore real estate, DHA Lahore, DHA Phase 6, 5 marla house). Each page needs unique local information: local landmarks, price ranges, schools, hospitals, transport. Google then reads you as the local expert and you start to rank for "near me" and city-specific searches. *My note:* every location page needs genuinely different content. Pages that only swap the place name are thin doorway pages and can hurt, so write the neighbourhood facts first and the listing second.

### 5. Technical foundation

| Problem | His fix |
|---|---|
| Slow loading (heavy property photos) | compress images (Smush), a caching plugin (WP Rocket) |
| No SSL | free SSL via Cloudflare |
| The same property on several pages | **canonical tags** |
| No schema | real-estate structured data with price, address and property type |
| Mobile | 54 to 60% of traffic is mobile, so design for the phone first |

His client case: **eight months of SEO with no result** at another agency; on audit they found **47 indexing errors in Search Console** plus other issues, fixed them, and traffic began to improve **within three weeks**. The lesson: check Search Console before producing more content. See [technical-seo-audit.md](technical-seo-audit.md).

### 6. Backlinks, in two phases

1. **Months 1 to 2, foundational:** Google Business Profile links, Pakistani property directories, social profiles, listings in the local chamber of commerce and business associations.
2. **From month 3, authority:** guest posts on property and real-estate blogs, coverage in local newspapers' digital editions, **data-driven content** such as "DHA Lahore property prices 2026", and appearances on real-estate and business podcasts.

His rule: **one relevant local backlink beats 50 generic ones.** (Earned or editorially placed links only; see [backlinks-and-offpage.md](backlinks-and-offpage.md).)

### How to use this for a real-estate client

1. Pull the keyword list with the four-sign test and keep only location plus buyer-intent phrases.
2. Fix the profile and the Search Console errors before writing.
3. Build the three content types, location hierarchy first.
4. Offer unique local data (prices, area guides) because it is both content and link bait.
5. Do not copy his numbers into a client proposal; use the client's own Search Console data once there is some.

*Skipped on purpose:* the free workshop announcement with its $1 entry fee, the training pitch and the seller phone number shown on screen.

## Restaurants (`PVjK7gtG9Ik`, "Stop Paying Food Influencers, Run This Restaurant SEO Strategy Instead", 2026)

Despite the title, the captions never compare the cost of influencers with SEO; the content is a local-SEO checklist for a physical restaurant. His framing: a restaurant survives on whether it shows at the top of the map when someone searches "fast food near me" or "desi restaurant near me". It will not show if the business is not on Google at all, if the profile is dead, if it has only 5 to 10 reviews, or if its name appears nowhere else on the web.

**His claims (unverified):** restaurants average **1,000 to 1,500 Google reviews**, which he says is 3 to 4 times other business types, so Google clearly treats reviews as central for restaurants. A Dubai Indian restaurant had 80 reviews with **no replies and old photos**; they added **40 fresh reviews from real customers in a month**, replied to all and updated the photos, and it reached the **map pack in two months with no backlinks**. A Lahore restaurant that struggled to get 10 to 15 customers a day saw **daily walk-ins grow 7 times in six months** from SEO alone. He also says profile optimisation by itself can deliver **40 to 50% of the result** from scratch.

### 1. Reviews and reputation (the strongest factor, his view)

Fresh, relevant and replied-to. Ask **every** customer politely, **reply to every review, positive or negative**, and let location words come naturally in what customers write ("best biryani in Gulberg"). *My note:* never script or incentivise what a reviewer says, and do not coach them to include keywords; that breaks Google's review policy. Make asking easy (a QR code or short link to the review form at the till) and ask at the happy moment. For the review-pattern red flags and the "no public review quality score" point, see [google-business-profile.md](google-business-profile.md).

### 2. Complete, accurate Google Business Profile

He says 90% of restaurants do not use it properly; it is "free real estate from Google".

- **Exact business name**, no stuffed keywords.
- **Category** chosen from Google's list ("Restaurant", "Pakistani restaurant", "Fast food restaurant").
- **Opening hours exact**, including closed days.
- **At least 20 high-quality photos**: interior, food, staff, exterior.
- **Menu links.**
- **Weekly posts.** He says posts are a ranking signal and that weekly updaters get about 30% more customer interactions. *My note:* Google has not confirmed posts as a ranking factor, so treat the 30% as his claim; posts are still cheap activity signals and useful for customers.

### 3. Local keywords and the website

Three keyword kinds:

| Type | Examples (his) |
|---|---|
| **Near me** | best restaurant near me, biryani near me, pizza delivery near me |
| **City** | best restaurants in Lahore, top cafes in Karachi, fine dining Islamabad |
| **Cuisine plus location** | Pakistani restaurants DHA Lahore, BBQ spots Gulberg, Chinese food F7 Islamabad |

He points to another video in which he ranked one page "within 7 minutes" live as proof that keyword choice is the lever (one demo, see [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md)).

Where to put them: **homepage H1** with the primary keyword; **meta title** with the restaurant name plus a keyword; **meta description** with location plus cuisine; the **About page** mentions the location naturally; the **footer carries the full NAP (name, address, phone) exactly as in the Google profile**; add **local schema markup** (Restaurant, with the menu) so Google sees the type, the area and the menu. Rank Math can generate this automatically. See [local-seo-keywords.md](local-seo-keywords.md).

### 4. Local content and citations

Owners say they have no time to write, so he limits it to **four kinds of post** on the site blog:

1. **Best [dish] in [area]** (informational, for example "best nihari in old Lahore"), and list your own restaurant in it.
2. **Menu highlights** ("new summer barbecue menu", "what is fresh this month").
3. **Event posts** ("Eid special buffet at [restaurant name]").
4. **A page per branch** if there are several, each carrying that area's keyword.

**Citations** mean being listed in local directories with the **identical name, address and phone** everywhere. His Pakistan list: **Foodpanda, TripAdvisor, a Facebook business page, Bing Places and local city directories.** The common mistake is a different phone or address on each platform: one of his clients had three addresses on three platforms, which confused Google and dropped rankings. Make every listing match the profile exactly.

### 5. Bonus: AI search for restaurants

Gemini, ChatGPT and Perplexity now recommend restaurants too. When someone asks "best restaurants in Lahore", your name appears only if the **profile has complete structured information, the site has FAQ schema, and the reviews are genuine and fresh**. He calls it new, so early implementers gain an edge. *My note:* FAQ rich results are now narrow in Google, so add FAQ markup for clarity, not for a promised result; see [geo-aeo-playbook.md](geo-aeo-playbook.md).

### How to use this for a restaurant or cafe

1. Fix the profile first (name, category, hours, photos, menu) and start the review habit; the other steps depend on it.
2. Make the name, address and phone identical on the website footer, the profile and every directory.
3. Add Restaurant schema and a menu page.
4. Publish one of the four post types a month; do not try to do more.
5. Track calls, direction requests and menu clicks from the profile, not site visits.

*Skipped on purpose:* the advanced-course pitch with its WhatsApp number and discount code.
