# Seven lessons from ten years, and a realistic timeline

Sources: "800+ Hours SEO Learning = These 7 SEO Steps" (`pYolbhgtLew`, 6 Jun, 5.1K views) and "11 Minutes of SEO That Took Me 11 Years to Learn" (`2mNFqOadVLY`, 4 Aug, 5.4K). Both read from YouTube's transcript panel in the original Hindi and translated by hand. The second is a compressed overview of research, on-page, technical and links that repeats what the other notes already cover (crawl, process, index, the quality-link test, semantic SEO); only what is new is kept here. His numbers are claims from his own sites and clients, not verified facts.

## The seven lessons (his order)

1. **Wrong keywords mean everything is wasted.** His first site targeted "digital marketing" and, after six months of content and on-page work, ranked nowhere, because the top five were Forbes, HubSpot and similar authority sites. His winning-keyword test, tighter than the one in [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md): in the top five Google results there is **one site under DR 30, one irrelevant result, one site younger than a year, and allintitle under 100**. He says to target such a keyword "with your eyes closed", even at 50 searches a month; it can rank very fast (he has shown a page doing it in 10 minutes).
2. **Low competition first, high volume later.** A 200-search keyword with no competition can rank in 30 days; a 10,000-search keyword held by Forbes-sized sites may not in two years. He says one client now makes about 3,000 to 5,000 pounds a month from SEO using only low-competition keywords. Search volume matters less than you think because one topic page collects traffic from many variations (his example: a page ranking for 1,900 keywords with 38,000-plus visits that was written for one). Once **10 to 20 low-competition pages rank**, move gradually to medium competition. Check that the intent is buying, competition is truly low, and the keyword is targeted as a topic.
3. **Topics rank, not keywords.** In 2014 you repeated a keyword 20 to 30 times; now that can be penalised. Read the top 3 to 5 competitors' headings and subtopics and write something about **ten times** better. Two jobs: topical mapping (cluster same-intent keywords into one page) and semantic writing (add the related terms a tool such as Frase, NeuronWriter or Surfer returns). See [topical-mapping-and-intent.md](topical-mapping-and-intent.md).
4. **Ignoring technical SEO is a time bomb.** It starts invisible and spreads "like a virus". His causes: pages made then deleted (404s waste crawl budget), JavaScript bloat, **1 MB, 2 MB or even 5 MB images**, Core Web Vitals problems, **duplicate content (the biggest problem on e-commerce sites)**. Watch it from day one: fast hosting, light theme, SSL, sitemap in Search Console, test on a real phone, do not make pages heavy for design's sake, and work to a day-by-day plan ([agency-30-day-plan.md](agency-30-day-plan.md)).
5. **Backlink quality, not quantity.** A client bought **200 links** from a local seller and the site did not rank in six months, while other clients rank with **zero paid links**. A quality link is from a relevant, aged site, in the body, from a page that gets traffic, with relevant anchor text. His preference: pick low-competition keywords that need no links; if you do build links, foundational ones first (social profiles, Web 2.0, forums, local citations), then from month 2 or 3 guest posts and niche edits from quality sites only. See [backlinks-and-offpage.md](backlinks-and-offpage.md).
6. **SEO is patience.** His first result took **10 months**; clients now see results in 3 to 4 months because the strategy is better. Anyone expecting results in 30 days and quitting loses. He repeats that 97% of sites get zero Google traffic (his figure, no source).
7. **Search Console is your friend.** Which pages rank, which are stuck, which queries have impressions but no clicks, indexing and Core Web Vitals errors, and you can submit new pages for indexing. His example: a page at **position 5 with 500 impressions a month but 10 clicks**; he rewrote the title tag and meta description and **CTR went from 2% to 8%** at no cost. Use it daily. See [grow-existing-site-without-new-content.md](grow-existing-site-without-new-content.md) and the `sc_triage.py` script for the same idea at scale.

## His realistic timeline

| Months | What happens (if the work is done right and without mistakes) |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Indexing, foundation, content |
| 3 to 4 | Rankings begin on low-competition keywords |
| 5 to 6 | Traffic starts to arrive, results are visible |

This is faster than the 6-to-12-month range in [operating-framework.md](operating-framework.md); treat his 3-to-6 months as the good case for a new site with well-chosen low-competition keywords and 8 to 10 months as typical.

## Five myths he busts (`nia1Wg7odxc`, "You'll Never Find SEO Hard Again After This Video", 1 Jun, 3.3K views)

Same material again in myth form, with a few details worth keeping:

| Myth | His answer |
|---|---|
| SEO takes one to two years | Competitive keywords do; untapped low-competition ones can rank in about 30 days (he says he ranked a live page in 10 minutes on video). 90% of sites that fail have the wrong keywords as the root cause: "Google is a machine, learn to drive it". |
| You cannot rank without backlinks | Backlinks matter, but not every keyword needs them; he shows low-link sites with strong traffic. They chose untapped keywords, built topical maps and content, and built topical authority. |
| You must be a technical expert | Five basics remove about half of technical problems: fast hosting (and a CDN), a light theme, a sitemap submitted in Search Console, HTTPS, a mobile-friendly theme. Fix the rest when Search Console reports it. |
| AI killed SEO | A client who joined in 2025 shows a year of growth; SEO has evolved, and people learning it now will pass those who wait to see. |
| You need expensive tools | The excuse is the price of Ahrefs or Semrush. Free options he lists: Search Console, a free keyword extension, a Semrush trial, Screaming Frog (free tier), Rank Math. (He also repeats the shared-seat suggestion; this playbook does not endorse it.) |

His three-step version: **(1) smart keyword research** with the three-part test above, **(2) topical, semantic content** (outline from the top 3 to 5 results, related terms used naturally, easy-to-read formatting, one good topical article can bring traffic from 1,000+ keywords), **(3) consistent publishing and patience**: **10 to 15 high-quality articles a month beat 50 average ones**, watch Search Console, improve pages that reach the top 10, and build backlinks only for pages that are already worth sending traffic to.
