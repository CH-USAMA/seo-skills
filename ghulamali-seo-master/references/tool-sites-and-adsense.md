# Tool websites and AdSense: his full process

Sources: "How to Make Tools Website ( Live Process )" (40K views), "How to Make a Tools Website For Adsense" (33K), "How to Rank Tool Site ( Complete Process )" (19K), plus the shorter "This Boring Website Makes $36K/Month" already summarised elsewhere. The three long videos tell the same story with slightly different intros. They were read from YouTube's transcript panel in the original Urdu/Hindi and translated by hand; the captions had no English version.

## The pitch, in his words

A "boring" business anyone can run part time (student, job holder, freelancer, housewife): build a **free online tool** (a calculator or similar), rank it in Google, earn from **AdSense**. No coding, no expertise needed. Three steps: find an untapped tool niche, build the tool with AI, get search traffic with SEO.

His examples (attribute them as his, shown on screen, not verified):

- One site with about 91,000 monthly visits, all on a single tool page, earning roughly $2,000 to $4,000 a month from AdSense.
- Another with about 82,000 visits and four pages; one with about 700,000 visits; one with about 250,000 visits earning "10 to 15 thousand" a month (the captions do not make the currency clear). The dollar signs in the captions next to traffic numbers are transcription artefacts; they mean visits.
- His own tool site earning about $400 a month, and a tool site he says he sold for PKR 4 million.
- His closing claim: the sites that work do so because everything on them was done deliberately with zero mistakes. SEO punishes even one mistake more than most things.

## Step 1: find an untapped tool niche (his best of 10+ methods)

Tool: Ahrefs Keywords Explorer (US database for these examples). Filters on the full keyword database:

1. **Lowest DR: 10.** Only keywords where one of the top five results is a site with DR 10 or lower.
2. **Include, any word:** the tool type, for example "calculator".
3. **Word count: minimum 4 words.**

Result in his demo: about 26,000 easy keywords. Then the manual check, which is the real test: search a few at random; if the top 10 contains a site with DR under 10 (or at most about 20), the keyword is a candidate.

**His improvement on keyword-by-keyword hunting:** find the **entire low-DR site** that already gets large traffic, because it proves a whole group of winnable keywords. Open that site's top pages. In his example a single tool page was taking about 43,000 visits a month from Google. Then build your own, better version of that tool (never copy their code or text).

He promises a list of ten untapped niches in the videos; those were shown on screen only and are not in the captions.

## Step 2: build the tool by "vibe coding"

Use an AI builder (Replit, Claude, ChatGPT). His workflow with Replit's free tier:

1. Sign in, open the chat, describe the tool in plain language.
2. Give a **competitor's URL** and ask the AI to explore it, note every feature, and build a similar tool.
3. Wait: his demo (a birth-chart style calculator) took about 7 minutes for a prototype and 12 to 14 minutes for a working app. The AI tests the tool itself by filling the form in a browser.
4. Customise by talking to it ("only this box, not that one").
5. **Publish.** Replit gives a URL on a sub-domain; a custom domain needs a paid plan.
6. On the WordPress site: add a **new page** and either paste the tool's code into the page body or embed the published URL. Done.

Practical caveats (not from him): test the tool yourself with real inputs, check it gives correct answers (a calculator that is wrong is worse than none), and keep the tool's code and layout your own.

## Step 3: SEO for the tool page

The sequence he gives, which matches [new-website-step-by-step.md](new-website-step-by-step.md):

1. **Niche and keywords**, as above.
2. **Semantic content** around the tool, written by a human or an AI. His rules for this kind of site: no keyword repetition, and **LLM optimisation does not work for tool sites**; write semantically optimised content on the topic. Example: for "BMI calculator", treat BMI as the topic and include the relevant NLP terms from a tool such as Surfer, NeuronWriter or Rankability. Give the writer the term list, the outline and an instruction to use the terms naturally. Details in [on-page-and-semantic-content.md](on-page-and-semantic-content.md).
3. **On-page**, a combination of about 20 small items: title tag, meta description, H1, subheadings, internal and external links, URL structure, image alt text, keywords above the fold, publish date, author bio, content quality, user intent, a video, secondary keywords. (He offers a full checklist by email; the list in [new-website-step-by-step.md](new-website-step-by-step.md) is the part he spells out.)
4. **Technical**, "from day one make no mistakes, because mistakes become technical issues slowly": HTTPS, no duplicate content, page speed (he says no more than 1 to 2 seconds here, 3 seconds in another video), mobile friendly, a reviewed robots.txt, structured data, no broken pages or deleted URLs left behind. See [technical-seo-audit.md](technical-seo-audit.md).
5. **Backlinks:** not required to rank, but they **secure** the ranking against a competitor who builds links. Types: Web 2.0, guest posts, forums, profiles; the important ones are guest posts and niche edits, which cost money. See [backlinks-and-offpage.md](backlinks-and-offpage.md).

## What he keeps for the paid workshop

Niche selection secrets, untapped keyword methods, AI-written content that ranks, advanced tool building and installation, **AdSense approval tricks**, design, and the on-page, technical and off-page detail. He puts the cost of building and ranking a tool site at roughly $100 to $200 and says the workshop is a few live Zoom classes. None of those details are in the videos, so do not invent them. For AdSense approval rely on Google's own AdSense program policies (original, useful content, privacy policy, clear navigation) and the `seo-site-audit` skill.

## How to use this for the user

- Treat his income figures as case studies.
- The part that transfers to any site is the **untapped-keyword filter plus manual DR check**, which also appears in [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md).
- Before recommending a tool site to the user, check that the niche is not already served by a free tool from a large brand, and that AdSense is allowed in their country and for that content.
