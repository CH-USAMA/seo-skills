# 30-day SEO + GEO roadmap for a new website

Source: "The 30-Day SEO+GEO Roadmap For New Websites" (9K views), with details from "How to start SEO+GEO from ZERO in 2026".

**Expectation setting (his words):** this is not a "rank number one in 30 days" plan; do not believe anyone who says that. A brand-new site is invisible. Google takes months to trust it; AI engines may start recommending it within weeks, **if** you do things right and in the right order. The 30 days set a foundation.

## Week 1: foundation and findability

The search engines must find and read you. One mistake here makes everything else wasted.

- [ ] Set up **Google Search Console**.
- [ ] Set up **Bing Webmaster Tools** (Bing feeds several AI search products).
- [ ] Generate an **XML sitemap** (he uses the SEOPress plugin) and submit it in Search Console.
- [ ] Check **robots.txt**: nothing blocking Google or the AI search crawlers.
- [ ] Speed: loads within about 3 seconds, mobile responsive, HTTPS.
- [ ] Decide what you want to be known for and who the audience is. Search related questions on Google and on AI platforms, and see which sites work.

## Week 2: core content

- [ ] Publish **5 to 10 pages** to start. Publishing 100 weak pages does not help; make fewer and make each the best answer.
- [ ] Follow four rules for every page:
  1. **Answer first**: define it in the first sentence, then explain.
  2. **Cover it completely**: study competitors, then add what none of them cover.
  3. **Structure for extraction**: clear headings, short paragraphs, lists, tables, a real FAQ.
  4. **Add evidence**: numbers, sources, expert quotes.
- [ ] **Internally link** pages to each other so none is orphaned.
- [ ] Add **schema markup**.

Pick the first keywords with [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md); write with [on-page-and-semantic-content.md](on-page-and-semantic-content.md).

## Week 3: the GEO layer (most AI citations come from here)

- [ ] Add short, self-contained **question-and-answer blocks** to the pages, even every page: a question heading and a tight answer. AI lifts these as they are.
- [ ] Start **off-site presence**: help in communities and forums where your audience is, get listed in relevant directories and "best of" roundups. Linked or unlinked both help.
- [ ] **Freshness**: put real dates on content and plan updates. For Google rankings site age and backlinks dominate; for AI citations, fresh and well-structured content matters most.

## Week 4: measure and loop

- [ ] Write **10 to 20 prompts** your customers would ask an AI. Test them in ChatGPT and Google AI Mode. Record where you are mentioned and where you are linked.
- [ ] In Search Console find pages with impressions, especially those on page 1 or 2 of Google.
- [ ] **Refresh loop**: for pages that are close to ranking or already surfacing in AI, find the weak spots and add depth, better answers, internal links, fresh dates. Repeat until you reach the target.

## Case-study fragment he gives

A client's ads were stopped to focus on organic. The first 30 days were wasted on mistakes, but in 6 to 7 weeks organic traffic arrived and the first sale came from it. His point: the order and the mistakes matter.

## Adapting this to an existing store (for example a WooCommerce shop)

The roadmap is for new sites. For an established store, use Weeks 1 and 4 as an audit (indexing, sitemap, robots, speed, then measure) and Week 3's Q&A blocks and freshness on category and top product pages. Check [combining-with-beyondseo.md](combining-with-beyondseo.md) for the evidence step first.
