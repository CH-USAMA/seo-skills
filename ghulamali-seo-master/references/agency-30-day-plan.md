# The agency 30-day plan, day by day

Source: "Leaked: The 30 Day SEO Plan Top Agencies Run on New Clients" (`QGkFpAJVpD8`, 20 May, 5.8K views). Read from YouTube's transcript panel in the original Hindi and translated by hand. It is the **tool-level, day-by-day** version of the plan in [new-site-30-day-roadmap.md](new-site-30-day-roadmap.md), which is the week-by-week version with a bigger GEO layer and a measuring loop. Use this one when you want to know what to do on which day; use the other for the reasoning. His numbers are claims from his agency work, not verified facts.

His headline: week 1 foundation and keyword research, week 2 content that can rank, week 3 on-page and technical, week 4 backlinks and AI search. He says he uses this on every client.

## Week 1: foundation and keywords

**Days 1 to 5, untapped keyword research.** The biggest mistake is spending time on high-competition keywords. A new site will never rank for "SEO services Lahore"; "SEO services for real estate Lahore" is winnable in 30 days. His example: one client's keyword targeting alone was fixed and the site now brings about $7,000 a month of sales from Google (his claim).

Ahrefs recipe: Keywords Explorer, main keyword, country, Matching terms, then two filters: **lowest DR 20** and **minimum 4 words**. Open a keyword in Google and keep it only if the top five results include **at least one low-DR site, at least one irrelevant result, and at least one site that is a year old or younger than a year**. Collect **30 to 50** of these. He says they need no money spent on backlinks.

*Tool note:* he suggests buying a cheap Ahrefs seat from a reseller. This playbook does not recommend shared seats (they break the tool's terms). Search Console and any legitimate keyword tool do the same job on a smaller scale. See [keyword-research.md](keyword-research.md).

**Days 6 and 7, foundation.** Fast hosting (he names SiteGround or a Cloudways-type cloud host), a **light theme** on WordPress (GeneratePress or Astra), SSL installed, site added to Search Console, **Rank Math** for basic SEO setup.

## Week 2: content that ranks

Google ranks **topics, not keywords**, and a page that covers a topic can rank for thousands of keywords. His example: one client page built for a single keyword now ranks for over 2,700 keywords in Ahrefs and earns about $6,000 (his claim).

**Days 8 to 10, outlines.** For each keyword, search it, open the top 3 to 5 results, scan their heading structure and build your outline from what they cover (and what they miss).

**Days 11 to 14, write.** Run the topic through a content-optimiser tool (he names Surfer, NeuronWriter, Frase and Rankability): it returns **200+ semantic terms** to use naturally. The finished page should be semantically optimised, easy to read, in active voice, in the reader's native language and better than the top competitors. His line: good content costs once and pays repeatedly for as long as the site exists. See [on-page-and-semantic-content.md](on-page-and-semantic-content.md).

## Week 3: on-page and technical

**Days 15 to 18, on-page, on every page:** keyword in the title tag, meta description, H1 and at least one H2; keyword in image alt text; internal links to related pages; external links to authority sources; an embedded video where it fits.

**Days 19 to 21, technical:**
- Speed: a caching plugin (WP Rocket), image compression (WP Smush), Cloudflare CDN.
- Mobile: he says about 62% of web traffic is mobile in 2026. (Google has retired the standalone Mobile-Friendly Test; use PageSpeed Insights or Lighthouse.)
- Indexing: Search Console **Page indexing** report (he calls it coverage), submit the sitemap, then submit the new pages with the **URL Inspection** tool.
- 404s: a broken-link checker plugin; fix each or redirect it to the closest relevant page.

## Week 4: backlinks and AI search

**Days 22 to 28, authority links.** He first picks keywords that do not need backlinks; links are for making the site stronger, ranking faster and stopping competitors from outranking you. Days 22 to 25: foundational links (social profiles, Web 2.0 sites, local citations). Days 26 to 28: forum and community links (the transcript is unclear, it reads "Reddit" and "foreign links"), plus **two or three guest posts by outreach**. His rule: **five quality links beat 50 or 100 spam links**. Community links only help if you take part for real; promotional drive-by posts get removed and banned. See [backlinks-and-offpage.md](backlinks-and-offpage.md).

**Days 29 and 30, AI search** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google, Gemini). Four tactics: a **direct answer in the first 100 words** (AI pulls from there); clear structure (H2 and H3 headings, bullets, short paragraphs); **schema markup** (Rank Math adds it); build **E-E-A-T** (author bio and credentials, a proper About page, case studies and results). He claims AI-optimised content converts 4.4 times better than normal SEO content; he gives no source, so do not repeat that number. See [geo-aeo-playbook.md](geo-aeo-playbook.md).

## How he positions it

This is the beginner layer. The advanced layer (crawling, indexing, ranking algorithms, advanced technical and link building) is what his paid training covers; skip the pitch and discount code.

## Using it

- **New site:** follow this plan for the order and the keyword filters, the roadmap note for the GEO measuring loop.
- **Existing site (for example a shop):** days 1 to 5's keyword test is still the best way to pick new pages; days 15 to 21 are an audit checklist for pages you already have.
