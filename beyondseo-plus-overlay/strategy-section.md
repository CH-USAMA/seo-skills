## Strategy layer (merged edition)

This edition adds a practitioner playbook distilled from Ghulam Ali's most-watched 2026 SEO and GEO videos, plus a combined workflow. Everything above and below this section is BeyondSEO as released; the added material only helps decide **what to do and in which order**, while BeyondSEO supplies the proof.

- For any site, start with [the combined workflow](playbooks/core/combined-workflow.md): preflight, evidence, prioritise, propose, apply safely, measure, keep site notes.
- To route by task (untapped keywords, topical maps, semantic content, local SEO and Google Business Profile, real-estate and restaurant sites, a 30-day plan, growing an existing site, getting cited by AI, online shops on Shopify or WooCommerce, new sites and tool sites), read [the playbook index](playbooks/ghulam-ali/README.md).
- With Search Console data, run `scripts/sc_triage.py` first and read [how to interpret it](references/search-console-triage.md).
- Lessons from real client work are in [field lessons](references/field-lessons.md); a per-site notes file is in [the template](assets/site-notes-template.md).
- His numbers are his claims, not verified facts; say "his method" when passing them on. Where he and Google disagree, Google's documented behaviour wins. BeyondSEO's no-guarantee rule applies throughout.
